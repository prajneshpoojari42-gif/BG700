How to Get Tuya Device ID & Local Key 5-Minute Guide
1

Pair with Smart Life or Bagotte App

Make sure your Bagotte BG700 is connected to your 2.4GHz Wi-Fi in the Smart Life or Tuya app.

2

Create a Free Tuya Developer Account

Go to iot.tuya.com and sign up for a free developer account. Go to Cloud > Development > Create Cloud Project.

3

Link Your Smart Life App Account

In your Tuya Cloud project, click Cloud > Link Tuya App Account, scan the QR code with your Smart Life app.

4

Run TinyTuya Wizard (Automated)

Run this simple one-liner in your terminal to automatically extract all Device IDs and Local Keys:

npx @tuyapi/cli wizard

Or using python: python -m tinytuya wizard
